# Project Context

This application is an AI Self-Reflection Coach focused on productivity enhancement. The app helps users reflect on their daily work, study, habits, focus, distractions, and unfinished tasks. Its main goal is to help users understand why they are not productive, identify controllable and uncontrollable blockers, and turn reflection into practical improvement plans.
